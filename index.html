          Sound familiar?
          
Your team is capable of much more.
The bottleneck isn’t people — it’s process.

          
Most small and mid-size businesses are sitting on hours of recoverable time every week. These are the patterns we see in almost every engagement.

        

        

          

            01
            
The same questions, answered over and over.

            
Your best people spend an hour a day answering questions a well-built system could handle instantly — customer inquiries, internal policy questions, onboarding FAQs.

          

          

            02
            
Knowledge lives in people’s heads.

            
When someone leaves, their expertise walks out with them. New hires take months to ramp up. There’s no single place where your team’s know-how actually lives.

          

          

            03
            
Data moves between tools by hand.

            
Someone is copying data from one system to another. Reports get assembled manually. Invoices wait. Handoffs get dropped. Your tools don’t talk to each other — and your team pays the price.

          

          

            04
            
You can’t respond after hours. Your competitors can.

            
Customers expect fast answers around the clock. You can’t staff for it — and without automation, every missed inquiry after 5pm is a missed opportunity.

The right AI system — built around your actual workflows, not a generic template — quietly eliminates hours of low-value work every week. We build those systems, connect them to your existing tools, and hand them over so you own them completely.

          The process
          
Simple by design. Fast by necessity.

          
We’ve run enough engagements to know the bottleneck is never the technology — it’s having a clear process. Here’s ours.

        

        

          

            
01

            
Audit

            
We spend time inside your operations — talking to your team, mapping your workflows, identifying where AI can have the biggest and fastest impact. No assumptions. No generic frameworks.

            Week 1
          

          

            
02

            
Build

            
We design and build the system, connecting AI to the tools you already use — Slack, Google Workspace, HubSpot, Notion, QuickBooks. You don’t need a new platform. We work inside your existing stack.

            Weeks 2–4
          

          

            
03

            
Hand off

            
We train your team, document how everything works, and hand over full ownership. You don’t need us to keep it running. We’ve succeeded when you can do it without us.

            Week 4+
