# Founder — Chris Wallace

## Role

Founder and principal practitioner at Pendium. Chris leads and delivers every engagement — clients work directly with the person who designed the system, not a project manager.

## Expertise

**AI assistant design** — Behavioral specification, persona architecture, guardrail design, prompt engineering, evaluation frameworks. Treats the assistant as a product with a user experience, not a chatbot bolted onto a form.

**Knowledge management systems** — RAG pipeline architecture, vector database design, document ingestion strategy, embedding models, retrieval interface design. Focus: accuracy and governance, not just capability.

**Design and tech automation** — Workflow analysis, AI pipeline design for creative and technical operations. Bridges design tools (Figma, Adobe) and engineering environments. Builds automation that creative and technical teams actually adopt — not just technically functional pipelines that sit unused.

## Philosophy

- AI implementation is an exercise in judgment, not just engineering.
- The best AI systems are designed from the user backward, not the model forward.
- Organizational AI fails most often at the handoff: between the system and the team that has to use it.
- Good systems are boring: they do exactly what they say, reliably, and teams forget they're there.

## Delivery Model

Solo practitioner at core. Trusted specialist collaborators (design, backend, data engineering) brought in as projects require. This keeps delivery senior while covering the full technical surface area of complex engagements.

Every engagement includes:
- Direct access to Chris throughout
- Weekly async updates + one live sync minimum
- Handoff documentation, runbook, and 30-day post-launch check-in

## Capacity

At full capacity:
- 2 active project engagements simultaneously
- 2–3 retainer clients
- 1–2 discovery sprints per month
