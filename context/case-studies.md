# Example Case Studies

Two illustrative engagements per vertical. Each demonstrates the shape, scope, and outcomes of a typical Pendium engagement.

---

## Technology & SaaS

### AI Support Assistant for a Developer Tools Company

**Challenge:** A 60-person developer tools company was losing engineering capacity to support. 30% of tickets were repeat questions already answered in documentation. Engineers were triaging before support staff even saw tickets.

**Engagement:** Eight-week project. Behavioral specification for a tiered support assistant — conversational for common questions, escalation-aware for edge cases. Trained on product documentation, changelog, and 18 months of closed tickets. Deployed in the customer Slack community and help center widget.

**Services:** AI Assistant Design & Deployment

**Outcome:** 65% deflection on tier-1 tickets in the first month. Engineers reclaimed ~15 hours/week. Customer satisfaction scores improved because common questions got instant, accurate answers instead of 48-hour wait times.

### Internal Knowledge System for a Fast-Scaling SaaS

**Challenge:** A B2B SaaS company growing from 40 to 120 employees over 18 months found that institutional knowledge was disappearing. Onboarding new engineers took 3+ months. The answer to most internal questions was "ask Sarah" — and Sarah was drowning.

**Engagement:** Ten-week project. Knowledge audit across Notion, Confluence, GitHub, and 2 years of Slack archives. RAG pipeline with structured ingestion, deduplication, and a freshness policy. Searchable AI interface integrated into Slack (`/ask-pendium`). Governance model defining who owns what and how content stays current.

**Services:** Knowledge Management Systems

**Outcome:** Engineer onboarding to productive contribution dropped from 12 weeks to 5. Sarah got her calendar back. "Ask Sarah" requests dropped 70%.

## Media & Publishing

### Archive Intelligence for a Digital Publisher

**Challenge:** A 12-year-old digital publisher had 180,000 articles. Traffic was concentrated on content published in the last 90 days. The archive was effectively invisible — to readers, to editorial staff, and to their own search. Years of original reporting was generating zero value.

**Engagement:** Twelve-week project. Full archive ingestion with embedding pipeline. Topic clustering and semantic similarity for related content recommendations. Editorial interface that let staff surface relevant archive pieces during story planning. Reader-facing "explore this topic" feature on article pages.

**Services:** Knowledge Management Systems, Design & Tech Automation

**Outcome:** Archive pageviews up 3x within 60 days. Editorial team surfacing relevant evergreen pieces in seconds instead of hours. Average session length increased 18% as readers followed related content paths.

### Editorial Workflow Automation for a Newsletter Company

**Challenge:** A newsletter company with four full-time staff was producing five newsletters per week. Roughly 40% of each production cycle was consumed by research synthesis, formatting, and distribution preparation — work that followed a clear pattern.

**Engagement:** Six-week project. Research assistant trained on source list, editorial voice, and topic briefs. Automated formatting pipeline that pulled from the research assistant output and applied the newsletter template. Integration with their email platform for pre-send checks and scheduling.

**Services:** Design & Tech Automation, AI Assistant Design & Deployment

**Outcome:** Production time per newsletter dropped from 6 hours to 2.5 hours. Team redirected saved time to original reporting and reader engagement. Subscriber satisfaction scores stable through the transition.

## E-Commerce & Retail

### Product Discovery Assistant for an Online Retailer

**Challenge:** A specialty retailer with 48,000 SKUs had a keyword search that frustrated customers. High-intent visitors were bouncing from product pages because they couldn't find the right product. Customer service was fielding "help me find" requests that should have been self-serve.

**Engagement:** Ten-week project. Semantic search layer over the full product catalog. Conversational discovery assistant that understood context ("something for a 6-year-old who likes dinosaurs under $40") and surfaced relevant products. Integrated with Shopify inventory for real-time availability.

**Services:** AI Assistant Design & Deployment, Knowledge Management Systems

**Outcome:** Add-to-cart rate up 22% for sessions that used the discovery assistant. "Help me find" customer service contacts down 18%. Average order value up 12% as the assistant suggested complementary items.

## Marketing & Agencies

### Creative Operations Automation for a Brand Agency

**Challenge:** A mid-size brand agency found that 60% of junior designer time was consumed by asset adaptation — taking approved master creative and resizing, reformatting, and checking it for brand compliance across deliverable sets. Work that was necessary but not creative.

**Engagement:** Seven-week project. Automated adaptation pipeline triggered from approved Figma frames. AI brand compliance checker that flagged deviations (color, typography, logo clearspace) before review. Output pipeline integrated with the agency's project management system.

**Services:** Design & Tech Automation

**Outcome:** Junior designer capacity available for creative work increased by 40%. Brand compliance errors caught before client review, eliminating embarrassing revision cycles. Client satisfaction scores improved as turnaround times dropped.

Campaign history, client preferences, and strategic context lived in CRM notes, Slack threads, email chains, and their own heads. ## Legal & Professional Services

### Matter Knowledge System for a Boutique Law Firm

**Challenge:** A 25-attorney boutique firm had ten years of matter files — briefs, memos, research, correspondence — stored in a folder structure that was nominally organized but practically unsearchable. Associates were re-researching questions that had already been answered. Partners were the lookup system.

**Engagement:** Fourteen-week project. Document intelligence pipeline over a decade of matter files. Semantic search with citation tracking — every AI response linked to the source document and page. Access controls matching the firm's existing matter confidentiality structure. Search interface available in the browser and via a Slack command.

**Services:** Knowledge Management Systems

**Outcome:** Associate research time for common question types cut by 35%. Partners could delegate more complex research with confidence that answers would be grounded in firm precedent. New associates became productive faster.

### Patient Education Automation for a Telehealth Company

**Challenge:** A telehealth company offering condition-specific care programs needed patient education content for 60+ condition categories. Clinical writers were the bottleneck — producing 2–3 pieces per week when 10–15 were needed. Content was also aging without a systematic update process.

## Education & EdTech

### Learner Support Assistant for an Online Learning Platform

**Challenge:** An online learning platform with 80,000 enrolled learners had a completion rate problem. Analysis showed the primary dropout trigger was learners getting stuck on concepts without timely help. The support team couldn't scale to the volume; forum responses averaged 18 hours.

**Engagement:** Ten-week project. AI tutor assistant trained on course content, video transcripts, common misconceptions by topic, and learning objectives. Socratic response design — the assistant guided learners toward answers rather than just providing them. Escalation to human tutors when learners were stuck after multiple exchanges. Available 24/7 in the course interface.

**Services:** AI Assistant Design & Deployment

**Outcome:** Learner completion rates up 18% in courses with the assistant deployed. Support ticket volume down 30%. Human tutors handling escalations reported the quality of questions improved — learners had already worked through the obvious blockers with the AI.

### Curriculum Intelligence System for a Corporate Training Company

**Challenge:** A corporate training company had a library of 3,000+ training modules — video, PDF, assessments — accumulated over 15 years. The library was hard to search, had significant content overlap, and was aging without systematic review. Clients were getting recommended outdated content.

**Engagement:** Twelve-week project. Full content library ingestion with metadata extraction and semantic indexing. AI-powered gap analysis that identified coverage holes and overlap. Content freshness scoring based on subject matter and industry update frequency. Recommendation engine for learning path construction. Internal tool for curriculum team to query and manage the library.

**Services:** Knowledge Management Systems, Design & Tech Automation

**Outcome:** Curriculum team identified 200+ modules needing update or retirement in days rather than the estimated months. Recommendation engine improved learner path completion rates by 24%. Clients received more relevant content; satisfaction scores improved.
