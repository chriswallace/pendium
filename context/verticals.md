# Target Verticals

Pendium serves eight verticals. All share common traits: they generate significant data and content, they have high-value human knowledge that's currently inaccessible at scale, and they have workflows that follow patterns AI can accelerate.

---

## 1. Technology & SaaS

**Slug:** technology-saas
**Who:** Product companies, developer tool businesses, platform operators, infrastructure companies, B2B SaaS

### Pain Points

- Engineering teams buried in support tickets that repeat the same questions
- New hire onboarding takes months because institutional knowledge is in Slack history and engineer heads
- Internal documentation exists but nobody can find the right thing at the right time
- Product teams want AI features but don't know how to build them without it becoming a distraction

### AI Opportunity

- AI support assistants that handle tier-1 and tier-2 tickets
- Internal knowledge systems that make the entire org as capable as the most senior people
- Embedded product AI features (copilots, suggestions, search)
- Engineering workflow automation (PR review, doc generation, test writing)

### Primary Service Fit

- AI Assistant Design & Deployment (support, internal, product-embedded)
- Knowledge Management Systems (internal docs, onboarding)
- Design & Tech Automation (dev workflow, code review, documentation)

## 2. Media & Publishing

**Slug:** media-publishing
**Who:** Digital publishers, news organizations, content studios, podcast networks, newsletter companies, trade publications

### Pain Points

- Large content archives that are invisible — traffic concentrated on <5% of content
- Editorial teams producing at volume with manual research and formatting workflows
- Audience personalization is wanted but implementation feels out of reach
- Metadata, tagging, and taxonomy are inconsistent, making search and recommendation unreliable

### AI Opportunity

- Archive intelligence: make years of content discoverable and linkable
- Editorial workflow automation: research, outline, formatting, distribution prep
- Reader-facing AI: personalized content discovery, topic-following assistants
- Metadata cleanup and enrichment at scale

### Primary Service Fit

- Knowledge Management Systems (archive intelligence, metadata)
- Design & Tech Automation (editorial production workflows)
- AI Assistant Design & Deployment (reader-facing tools)

---

## 3. E-Commerce & Retail

**Slug:** ecommerce-retail
**Who:** Online retailers, D2C brands, marketplaces, omnichannel retailers

### Pain Points

- Large product catalogs that are hard to search and navigate
- High customer service volume on questions that have known answers
- Creative team bottlenecked on producing asset variants for every channel and SKU
- Personalization is expected by customers but hard to deliver without a data team

### AI Opportunity

- Conversational product discovery and semantic search
- AI customer support for order, product, and returns questions
- Creative asset automation: ad variants, email banners, PDP imagery, copy variations
- Personalized product recommendation and merchandising

### Primary Service Fit

- AI Assistant Design & Deployment (customer support, product discovery)
- Design & Tech Automation (creative ops, asset production)
- Knowledge Management Systems (product catalog intelligence)

---

## 4. Marketing & Agencies

**Slug:** marketing-agencies
**Who:** Creative agencies, performance marketing firms, brand studios, in-house brand teams at large companies

### Pain Points

- Junior staff time consumed by asset resizing, format adaptation, and brand compliance checking
- Account managers context-switching between many clients with fragmented knowledge
- Brief-to-execution cycle is slow because research and ideation are manual
- Clients want AI-augmented deliverables but agencies don't know where to start

### AI Opportunity

- Creative operations automation: adapt assets across formats and channels automatically
- AI brand compliance checker: flag deviations before they reach review
- Client intelligence assistants: brief-aware, campaign-history-aware copilots for account teams
- AI-augmented creative strategy: research synthesis, concept generation, competitive analysis

### Primary Service Fit

- Design & Tech Automation (creative ops, brand compliance)
- AI Assistant Design & Deployment (client-facing, internal account management)
- Knowledge Management Systems (client knowledge, campaign history)

---

## 5. Legal & Professional Services

**Slug:** legal-professional
**Who:** Law firms, management consulting firms, accounting firms, boutique advisory practices

### Pain Points

- Institutional knowledge exists only in partner heads and unstructured file systems
- Associates spend hours on research that senior people could answer in minutes
- Client-ready deliverables take too long because drafting is done from scratch
- Matter and engagement history is not accessible or searchable at scale

### AI Opportunity

- Matter/engagement knowledge systems: make case history, precedents, and client knowledge searchable
- Research acceleration: AI that synthesizes large document sets and flags the relevant sections
- Drafting assistants: trained on firm templates, style, and past deliverables
- Client-facing tools: AI-augmented deliverables as a competitive differentiator

### Primary Service Fit

- Knowledge Management Systems (matter files, client history, precedents)
- AI Assistant Design & Deployment (research, drafting, client-facing)
- Design & Tech Automation (report production, document formatting)

## 6. Financial Services

**Slug:** financial-services
**Who:** RIAs, wealth management firms, FinTech startups, insurance companies, credit unions, family offices

### Pain Points

- Investment and research teams spending too much time gathering and synthesizing information
- Compliance documentation is a constant burden that diverts skilled staff from higher-value work
- Client communication is high-volume and repetitive but must be accurate and compliant
- Onboarding new advisors or analysts takes months due to institutional knowledge gaps

### AI Opportunity

- Research intelligence platforms: automated synthesis of earnings calls, filings, news, and data feeds
- Compliance documentation assistants: AI-drafted policies with human review workflow
- Client communication templates: personalized, compliant, fast
- Advisor knowledge systems: make the firm's investment philosophy and history accessible

### Primary Service Fit

- Knowledge Management Systems (research, compliance, institutional knowledge)
- AI Assistant Design & Deployment (research, client communication, advisor tools)
- Design & Tech Automation (report generation, compliance workflows)

---

## 7. Healthcare & HealthTech

**Slug:** healthcare-healthtech
**Who:** Digital health platforms, telehealth companies, health systems, medical device companies, pharma tech, wellness apps

### Pain Points

- Clinical and customer success teams fielding complex, high-stakes questions that require accurate answers
- Patient education content is expensive to produce and hard to personalize
- Clinical documentation is time-consuming and takes practitioners away from patient care
- Regulatory and compliance requirements make AI adoption feel risky without a thoughtful framework

### AI Opportunity

- Clinical support assistants: trained on product documentation and evidence base, with careful guardrails
- Patient education automation: condition-specific content at scale with clinical review workflow
- Internal knowledge systems for clinical and ops teams
- Documentation assistance: structured note generation with human review

### Primary Service Fit

- AI Assistant Design & Deployment (clinical support, patient-facing, with strong guardrails)
- Knowledge Management Systems (clinical documentation, internal knowledge)
- Design & Tech Automation (content production pipelines, documentation workflows)

---

## 8. Education & EdTech

**Slug:** education-edtech
**Who:** Online learning platforms, corporate training companies, universities, bootcamps, professional development providers

### Pain Points

- Learners get stuck and drop out because timely, personalized help is unavailable at scale
- Large content libraries (video, PDFs, assessments) are hard to search and become outdated
- Curriculum development is slow and expensive; updating existing content is a bottleneck
- Instructors and support staff are overwhelmed; high staff-to-learner ratios hurt margins

### AI Opportunity

- AI tutor assistants trained on course content, common misconceptions, and learning objectives
- Content intelligence: make the full library searchable and generate gap analyses
- Curriculum assistance: AI-aided content drafting with instructor review
- Learner personalization: adaptive pathways based on progress and engagement

### Primary Service Fit

- AI Assistant Design & Deployment (learner support, tutoring, instructor tools)
- Knowledge Management Systems (content library intelligence, curriculum knowledge)
- Design & Tech Automation (content production, curriculum update workflows)
