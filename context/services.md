# Services

Pendium offers three focused service lines. Each sells standalone; they compound when combined.

---

## 1. AI Assistant Design & Deployment

### What it is

End-to-end design and deployment of custom AI assistants — for internal teams, customer-facing products, or both.

### Who needs it

- Companies building internal copilots (support, ops, sales, legal, onboarding)
- Publishers and platforms wanting AI-powered user experiences
- SaaS products needing embedded AI features
- Teams replacing high-volume manual triage with intelligent routing

### What we deliver

- Behavioral specification: persona, tone, escalation rules, guardrails, refusal handling
- Prompt architecture and system design
- Integration with existing tools (Slack, Notion, Intercom, CRMs, custom APIs)
- Evaluation framework and quality benchmarking
- Handoff documentation and iteration playbook

### Why this fails elsewhere

Most AI assistants fail because they're built without behavioral design discipline. Prompt engineering without product thinking produces assistants that are technically functional but user-hostile. Pendium treats the assistant as a product.

### Engagement types

| Type | Scope | Price Range |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint | Audit current state, map opportunity, produce roadmap | $8K–$15K |
| Project | Full design, build, integration, testing, handoff | $30K–$80K |
| Retainer | Iteration, expansion, monitoring, new use cases | $8K–$18K/mo |

## 2. Knowledge Management Systems

### What it is

Building the infrastructure that makes organizational knowledge findable, trustworthy, and AI-accessible.

### Who needs it

- Media companies with large content archives that are invisible to users and staff
- Tech companies with sprawling internal documentation that slows onboarding
- Fast-scaling teams losing institutional knowledge as they grow
- Professional services firms with years of case/matter files locked in folders
- Any organization where "ask a senior person" is the primary search interface

### What we deliver

- Knowledge audit and taxonomy design
- RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipeline architecture
- Vector database setup and embedding strategy (OpenAI, Cohere, local models)
- Document ingestion, chunking, and update pipelines
- Search and retrieval interfaces (internal tools, Slack bots, APIs)
- Governance model: ownership, freshness policy, update workflows

### Why this fails elsewhere

Knowledge management done wrong creates AI that confidently says wrong things. Most implementations optimize for coverage (index everything) without designing for accuracy, freshness, or trust. Pendium designs the governance layer alongside the technical layer.

### Engagement types

| Type | Scope | Price Range |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint | Audit, taxonomy design, architecture recommendation | $8K–$15K |
| Project | Full pipeline build, interface, governance model | $40K–$120K |
| Retainer | Ongoing content ingestion, quality monitoring, expansion | $10K–$25K/mo |

## 3. Design & Tech Automation

### What it is

Automating repetitive, high-volume workflows in creative and technical operations using AI.

### Who needs it

- Design teams spending hours on asset resizing, copy variations, and brand compliance checks
- Engineering teams doing manual review, documentation generation, or QA workflows
- Media companies producing content at volume
- Marketing teams managing creative operations across many channels and formats
- Any team where the bottleneck is humans doing work that follows a pattern

### What we deliver

- Workflow analysis and automation opportunity mapping
- AI pipeline design (image, copy, code, data)
- Integration with design tools (Figma, Adobe CC, Canva), CMSs, and dev environments
- Custom tooling where off-the-shelf doesn't fit
- Training and adoption support for the teams using the new workflows

### Why this fails elsewhere

Most automation firms know engineering or design, not both. Tools built without design-team input sit unused. Pendium bridges the two: automation that creative and technical teams actually adopt.

### Engagement types

| Type | Scope | Price Range |
|---|---|---|
| Automation Audit | Map current workflows, identify highest-ROI automation targets | $5K–$10K |
| Project | Full pipeline design, build, integration, and rollout | $25K–$80K |
| Retainer | Ongoing pipeline maintenance, new workflow builds, training | $8K–$15K/mo |

## Engagement Shapes

### Discovery Sprint
**Duration:** 2 weeks
**Output:** Current-state audit, AI opportunity map, prioritized recommendations, implementation roadmap
**Best for:** Companies that want clarity before committing to a larger engagement. Almost always leads to follow-on work.

### Project Engagement
**Duration:** 6–16 weeks
**Output:** Defined scope, fixed outcome, production-ready system with handoff documentation
**Best for:** Companies with a specific, defined problem to solve.

### Embedded Retainer
**Duration:** Ongoing (3-month minimum)
**Output:** Pendium operates as a fractional AI implementation team — iteration, new initiatives, maintenance, strategic guidance
**Best for:** Companies that want a sustained AI capability partner, not a one-time build.

### Workshop / Training
**Duration:** Half-day or full-day
**Output:** Team upskilling on AI adoption, prompt engineering, knowledge management fundamentals, or automation strategy
**Best for:** Teams that want to build internal capability alongside external implementation.
**Price:** $3K–$8K
